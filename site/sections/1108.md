## Summary

Requires every agency to report all its categorical exclusions to CEQ within 30 days and each new one within 7 days; ([p. 62, line 12 to p. 62, line 15](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=62)) CEQ keeps a public register on its website. ([p. 63, line 6 to p. 63, line 8](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=63)) An agency may adopt another agency's exclusion after consulting it and documenting the adoption. ([p. 64, line 9 to p. 64, line 10](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=64))

## Key points

- Government-wide public register of categorical exclusions at CEQ ([p. 63, line 6 to p. 63, line 8](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=63))
- Streamlined adoption of other agencies' exclusions ([p. 63, line 16 to p. 63, line 19](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=63))

### Current law

FRA 2023 section 109 already allows adoption of another agency's exclusion after consultation and public notice; the CEQ register and notification deadlines are new.

### EPRA 2024 (reported S. 4753)

Not in EPRA 2024.

### SPEED Act (House-passed H.R. 4776)

The SPEED Act (sec. 2(e)) changed one phrase so agencies could adopt exclusions enacted by Congress. The CEQ register and the 30-day and 7-day reporting duties are Senate additions.
