## Summary

Authorizes $200 million a year for deposit in the Historic Preservation Fund for fiscal years 2027 through 2036. ([p. 414, line 10 to p. 414, line 11](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=414)) Deposits become available the next fiscal year without a further appropriation. ([p. 414, line 17 to p. 414, line 19](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=414)) Creates a $20 million-a-year grant program (fiscal years 2027 through 2030) for tribal historic preservation officers to build online databases of records and of properties of traditional religious or cultural importance, with protections against disclosing sensitive site locations. ([p. 417, line 1 to p. 417, line 2](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=417))

## Key points

- $200 million a year for the Historic Preservation Fund through 2036 ([p. 414, line 10 to p. 414, line 11](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=414))
- $20 million a year for tribal preservation databases ([p. 417, line 1 to p. 417, line 2](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=417))
