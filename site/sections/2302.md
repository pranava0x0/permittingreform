## Summary

Authorizes $200 million a year for deposit in the Historic Preservation Fund for fiscal years 2027 through 2036. Deposits become available the next fiscal year without a further appropriation. Creates a $20 million-a-year grant program (fiscal years 2027 through 2030) for tribal historic preservation officers to build online databases of records and of properties of traditional religious or cultural importance, with protections against disclosing sensitive site locations.
