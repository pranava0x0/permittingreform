## Summary

Adds Federal Power Act section 216A: work that maintains or increases grid capacity inside an existing right-of-way (reconductoring, higher operating voltage, added circuits, advanced transmission technologies, added storage at an existing facility, a parallel circuit) is categorically excluded from NEPA, is not a major federal action, and is not an undertaking under the National Historic Preservation Act. ([p. 201, line 23 to p. 202, line 2](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=201)) It also amends Natural Gas Act section 7 so that repair, replacement, looping, compression and capacity-enhancement of interstate gas pipelines inside an existing right-of-way or previously reviewed footprint are not major federal actions or historic-preservation undertakings. ([p. 203, line 12 to p. 203, line 15](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=203))

## Key points

- Reconductoring and in-corridor grid upgrades exempt from NEPA and NHPA section 106 ([p. 201, line 23 to p. 202, line 2](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=201))
- Gas pipeline looping and compression in existing corridors exempt from NEPA and NHPA section 106 ([p. 204, line 17 to p. 204, line 21](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=204))
- Clean Water Act and Clean Air Act requirements for pipelines stay in place ([p. 205, line 1 to p. 205, line 3](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=205))

- [Energy Permitting Reform Act of 2024 (S. 4753), reported in Senate](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/BILLS-118s4753rs/html/BILLS-118s4753rs.htm)

### Current law

DOE and other agencies have administrative categorical exclusions for some reconductoring; there is no statutory exclusion, and pipeline looping and compression get FERC environmental review.

### EPRA 2024 (reported S. 4753)

EPRA 2024 (sec. 209) only told Interior and Agriculture to adopt categorical exclusions for in-corridor grid work, which survives as section 2214. The statutory exemptions here, including the gas pipeline one, are new.

### SPEED Act (House-passed H.R. 4776)

Not in the SPEED Act.
