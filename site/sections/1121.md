## Summary

Replaces NEPA section 209 with an eNEPA technology mandate. ([p. 74, line 20 to p. 74, line 21](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=74)) Within 60 days CEQ must publish data standards for permitting data, within 120 days guidance on minimum functional requirements (automated project screening, case management, GIS tools, AI-assisted comment analysis, administrative-record tools), and agencies must begin implementing within 180 days. ([p. 76, line 2 to p. 76, line 5](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=76)) CEQ and the key permitting agencies build an ePermit unified interagency data system and a public authorization portal hosted by GSA. ([p. 89, line 20 to p. 89, line 23](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=89))

A pilot is due within a year and the system by December 1, 2028, 'to the maximum extent practicable'. ([p. 92, line 19 to p. 92, line 21](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=92)) Congress gets access to the data, fine-tuning procedures and prompt configurations agencies create or adapt for AI tools, but not proprietary or general pre-training materials. ([p. 90, line 23 to p. 91, line 4](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=90)) CEQ also sets outcome-based performance measures for agencies and staff. ([p. 92, line 24 to p. 93, line 2](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=92)) Authorizes $5 million a year for NEPA. ([p. 96, line 2 to p. 96, line 3](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=96))

## Key points

- Government-wide permitting data standards within 60 days ([p. 76, line 2 to p. 76, line 5](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=76))
- Public ePermit portal hosted by GSA, targeted for December 1, 2028 ([p. 92, line 18 to p. 92, line 21](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=92))
- AI-assisted comment analysis and screening tools named in statute; Congress gets the prompt configurations ([p. 90, line 22 to p. 91, line 1](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=90))
- Agency and personnel performance measures tied to the Permitting Improvement Fund ([p. 94, line 15 to p. 94, line 18](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=94))

### Current law

The 2023 law ordered CEQ to study a permitting portal. This section orders one built.

### EPRA 2024 (reported S. 4753)

Not in EPRA 2024.

### SPEED Act (House-passed H.R. 4776)

The House passed a separate ePermit Act alongside the SPEED Act in December 2025 with similar goals; the SPEED Act itself did not include it.
