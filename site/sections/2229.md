## Summary

Rewrites section 390 of the Energy Policy Act of 2005. ([p. 349, line 2 to p. 349, line 3](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=349)) The oil and gas activities listed in section 390(b) of the Energy Policy Act of 2005 are declared not to be major federal actions at all, rather than categorical exclusions. ([p. 349, line 9 to p. 349, line 11](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=349)) Geothermal is added to two of them, and a new item covers geothermal power lines of up to 20 miles in a corridor approved within the past 10 years. ([p. 350, line 7 to p. 350, line 11](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=350)) One listed item's look-back goes from 5 to 10 years, and geothermal gets a new item for power lines up to 20 miles in a corridor approved within 10 years. ([p. 350, line 1 to p. 350, line 2](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=350)) Drilling permits for these activities carry no NEPA requirements. ([p. 351, line 6 to p. 351, line 8](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=351))

## Key points

- Section 390 oil, gas and now geothermal activities are outside NEPA entirely ([p. 349, line 9 to p. 349, line 12](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=349))
- Drilling permits for those activities 'shall not have any requirements' under NEPA ([p. 351, line 6 to p. 351, line 8](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=351))

- [Energy Permitting Reform Act of 2024 (S. 4753), reported in Senate](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/BILLS-118s4753rs/html/BILLS-118s4753rs.htm)

### Current law

Section 390 creates a rebuttable presumption that a categorical exclusion applies to five oil and gas activities; extraordinary circumstances review has been contested.

### EPRA 2024 (reported S. 4753)

EPRA 2024 (sec. 208(f)) extended the section 390 categorical exclusions to geothermal. Declaring the activities not major federal actions, and stripping NEPA from the drilling permits, goes further and applies to oil and gas as well.

### SPEED Act (House-passed H.R. 4776)

Not in the SPEED Act.
