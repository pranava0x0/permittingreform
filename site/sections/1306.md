## Summary

Requires the Secretary to accept and integrate state-collected data in listing and delisting decisions when it meets the best-available-data standard. ([p. 155, line 14 to p. 155, line 17](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=155))

Section 4 of the Endangered Species Act of 1973 (16 U.S.C. 1533) is amended by adding at the end the following:

“(j) STATE DATA.—In making a determination to list, or delist, a species as a threatened species or an endangered species under this section, the Secretary shall accept and integrate State-collected data, subject to the condition that the State-collected data meets the definition of ‘best scientific and commercial data available’.”.
