## Summary

Definitions for the permitting tracker subtitle. ([p. 96, line 11 to p. 96, line 11](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=96)) A 'transparency project' is any action that needs an EIS, or an environmental assessment still unfinished 13 months past its deadline. ([p. 97, line 11 to p. 97, line 14](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=97))

In this subtitle:

[p. 96, line 12](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=96) (indent level 2)

(1) APPLICABLE DEADLINES.—The term “applicable deadlines” means the deadlines described in section 107(g) of the National Environmental Policy Act of 1969 (42 U.S.C. 4336a(g)).

[p. 96, line 16](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=96) (indent level 2)

(2) APPLICABLE SCHEDULE.—The term “applicable schedule” means the schedule established for a transparency project under subsection (a)(1)(C)(iv) of section 107 of the National Environmental Policy Act of 1969 (42 U.S.C. 4336a).

[p. 96, line 21](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=96) (indent level 2)

(3) NEPA TERMS.—The terms “authorization”, “Chairman”, “Council”, “environmental assessment”, “environmental impact statement”, “environmental review”, “key permitting agency”, “lead agency”, “project sponsor”, and “statement of purpose and need” have the meanings given those terms in section 3 of the National Environmental Policy Act of 1969.

[p. 97, line 3](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=97) (indent level 2)

(4) TRACKER.—The term “Tracker” means the Environmental Review and Permitting Tracker required under section 1132.

[p. 97, line 6](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=97) (indent level 2)

(5) TRANSPARENCY PROJECT.—The term “transparency project” means—

[p. 97, line 8](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=97) (indent level 3)

(A) a proposed agency action requiring the preparation of an environmental impact statement; or

[p. 97, line 11](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=97) (indent level 3)

(B) a proposed agency action requiring the preparation of an environmental assessment that has not been completed by the date that is 13 months after the deadline established for the project under paragraph (1)(A) of section 107(g) of the National Environmental Policy Act of 1969 (42 U.S.C. 4336a(g)).
