## Summary

Directs Interior and Agriculture to adopt, within 180 days, categorical exclusions for placing power lines in approved corridors, for upgrades and reconductoring of existing lines in existing rights-of-way or on previously disturbed land, and for battery storage on previously disturbed land.

## Key points

- Categorical exclusions for grid work on federal land within 180 days ([p. 304, line 23 to p. 305, line 1](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=304))

[p. 304, line 18](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=304) (indent level 1)

(a) DEFINITION OF PREVIOUSLY DISTURBED OR DEVELOPED.— In this section, the term “previously disturbed or developed” has the meaning given the term in section 1021.410(g)(1) of title 10, Code of Federal Regulations (or successor regulations).

[p. 304, line 23](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=304) (indent level 1)

(b) RULEMAKING.—Not later than 180 days after the date of enactment of this Act, to facilitate timely permitting, the Secretary of the Interior and the Secretary of Agriculture shall each develop or adopt 1 or more categorical exclusions, including allowing for extraordinary circumstances under which the categorical exclusion shall not be available, under the National Environmental Policy Act of 1969 (42 U.S.C. 4321 et seq.) for the following activities:

[p. 305, line 7](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=305) (indent level 2)

(1) Placement of an electric transmission or distribution facility in an approved right-of-way corridor, including a corridor designated under section 368 of the Energy Policy Act of 2005 (42 U.S.C. 15926).

[p. 305, line 12](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=305) (indent level 2)

(2) Any repair, maintenance, replacement, upgrade, modification, optimization, or minor relocation of, or addition to, an existing electric transmission or distribution facility or associated infrastructure, including electrical substations, within an existing right-of-way or on otherwise previously disturbed or developed land, including reconductoring and installation of grid-enhancing technologies.

[p. 305, line 20](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=305) (indent level 2)

(3) Construction, operation, upgrade, or decommissioning of a battery or other energy storage technology on previously disturbed or developed land.
