## Summary

Creates a Permitting Improvement Fund at Treasury that CEQ can transfer to permitting agencies for detailees, training, project managers, standardized processes, and public engagement. Authorizes $20 million a year for fiscal years 2027 through 2033.

## Key points

- $20 million a year, FY2027-2033, spent at CEQ's discretion ([p. 101, line 17 to p. 101, line 18](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=101))

[p. 101, line 7](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=101) (indent level 1)

(a) ESTABLISHMENT.—There is established in the Treasury of the United States a fund, to be known as the “Permitting Improvement Fund” (referred to in this section as the “Fund”).

[p. 101, line 11](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=101) (indent level 1)

(b) PURPOSE.—The purpose of the Fund is to improve the performance of key permitting agencies with respect to timely environmental reviews and authorizations required under the National Environmental Policy Act of 1969 (42 U.S.C. 4321 et seq.).

[p. 101, line 16](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=101) (indent level 1)

(c) TRANSFER AUTHORITY.—Amounts in the Fund shall be available to the Chairman to transfer to a key permitting agency for 1 or more uses described in subsection (d).

[p. 101, line 20](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=101) (indent level 1)

(d) USE OF AMOUNTS.—Amounts transferred under subsection (c) shall be used by a key permitting agency—

[p. 101, line 22](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=101) (indent level 2)

(1) to support interagency detailee and rotation opportunities;

[p. 101, line 24](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=101) (indent level 2)

(2) to facilitate professional development;

[p. 102, line 1](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=102) (indent level 2)

(3) to enhance support for agency project managers;

[p. 102, line 3](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=102) (indent level 2)

(4) to support standardizing processes among regional and field offices;

[p. 102, line 5](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=102) (indent level 2)

(5) to improve opportunities for public engagement; or

[p. 102, line 7](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=102) (indent level 2)

(6) for any other use, as determined by the Chairman, consistent with the purpose of the Fund.

[p. 102, line 9](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=102) (indent level 1)

(e) EFFECT ON DECISIONMAKING.—The use of funds under subsection (d) by a key permitting agency shall not impact the impartial decisionmaking of the key permitting agency with respect to environmental reviews or authorizations, either procedurally or substantively.

[p. 102, line 14](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=102) (indent level 1)

(f) AUTHORIZATION OF APPROPRIATION.—There is authorized to be appropriated to the Fund $20,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2027 through 2033.
