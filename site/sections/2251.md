## Summary

Amends the offshore renewable energy section of the Outer Continental Shelf Lands Act. Interior's duty changes from 'prevention of interference with reasonable uses' of the ocean to 'prevention of unreasonable interference with other uses'. ([p. 363, line 15 to p. 363, line 18](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=363)) Interior, in consultation with the Secretary of Commerce, may grant rights-of-way for electric transmission through national marine sanctuaries, with sanctuary permits lasting as long as the right-of-way. ([p. 364, line 4 to p. 364, line 6](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=364)) The Inflation Reduction Act's link between offshore wind leasing and oil and gas lease sales is preserved. ([p. 364, line 22 to p. 364, line 24](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=364))

## Key points

- Offshore wind standard changes to 'unreasonable interference' ([p. 363, line 16 to p. 363, line 18](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=363))
- Transmission cables allowed through marine sanctuaries ([p. 364, line 7 to p. 364, line 10](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=364))

### Current law

Section 8(p)(4)(I) requires prevention of interference with reasonable uses of the exclusive economic zone, the high seas and the territorial seas.

### EPRA 2024 (reported S. 4753)

The three Outer Continental Shelf Lands Act amendments come from EPRA 2024 (sec. 302(d)), with one change: sanctuary rights-of-way are no longer limited to electricity from renewable energy. EPRA's mandates do not appear here: one offshore oil and gas lease sale and one offshore wind lease sale a year from 2025 to 2029, 400,000 acres of wind leasing a year, and a 30-gigawatt offshore wind goal.

### SPEED Act (House-passed H.R. 4776)

Not in the SPEED Act.
