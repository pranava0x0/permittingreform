## Summary

Calculates the reduced early-years geothermal royalty per generating facility, starting from each facility's in-service date, so a second plant on an old lease gets its own 10-year lower-royalty period. ([p. 328, line 22 to p. 328, line 25](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=328))

## Key points

- Royalty step-up clock runs per power plant, not per lease ([p. 328, line 22 to p. 328, line 25](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=328))

## Selected statutory quotes

[p. 328, line 22 to p. 328, line 25](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=328)

The lower royalty period runs from each plant's in-service date.
