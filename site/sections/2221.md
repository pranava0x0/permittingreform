## Summary

Requires a categorical exclusion within 1 year for geothermal observation test wells on existing leases that disturb no more than 10 acres, use no permanent roads, and finish within a year. ([p. 324, line 17 to p. 324, line 20](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=324)) Casual-use activities need only 30 days' notice and are not major federal actions. ([p. 325, line 14 to p. 325, line 17](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=325))

## Key points

### Current law

BLM adopted administrative geothermal exploration exclusions in 2024 and 2025; no statutory one.

### EPRA 2024 (reported S. 4753)

EPRA 2024 (sec. 208(a)) required a categorical exclusion for geothermal test activity under 10 acres within 180 days. This bill gives 1 year, defines the test projects in detail, and separately excludes them from NEPA as non-major actions (section 1102).

### SPEED Act (House-passed H.R. 4776)

Not in the SPEED Act.
