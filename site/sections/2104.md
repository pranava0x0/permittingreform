## Summary

Adds Federal Power Act section 226. ([p. 218, line 16 to p. 218, line 18](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=218)) One or more states may refer a utility to FERC for an investigation of its local transmission planning. ([p. 220, line 6 to p. 220, line 10](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=220)) FERC forms an investigative team with up to 3 state appointees. ([p. 221, line 14 to p. 221, line 15](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=221)) If the team finds the utility's process is not selecting the more efficient or cost-effective local projects, or is crowding out regional planning, FERC may order process changes, cut the utility's return on equity on local projects, remove the presumption of prudence, or fine the utility. ([p. 223, line 1 to p. 223, line 3](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=223)) A fine, paid to the referring state, can reach $5 million for each local facility developed or energized from 60 days before the referral that FERC finds imprudent and harmful to that state's customers. ([p. 223, line 20 to p. 223, line 22](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=223))

## Key points

- States can trigger FERC investigations of local transmission spending ([p. 220, line 6 to p. 220, line 10](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=220))
- Remedies include lower return on equity and fines up to $5 million per facility ([p. 223, line 7 to p. 223, line 9](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=223))

“(B) the cost of which is recovered entirely from transmission customers within that service territory.

“(3) REFERRING STATE.— The term ‘referring State’ means 1 or more States making a referral under subsection (b).

“(4) REGIONAL TRANSMISSION FACILITY.—The term ‘regional transmission facility’ means an electric transmission facility the cost of which is recovered from transmission customers located within more than 1 service territory but within a single transmission planning region.

[p. 219, line 18](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=219) (indent level 2)

“(5) TRANSMISSION PLANNING REGION.—The term ‘transmission planning region’ means a region for which the Commission determines that electric transmission planning is appropriate, such as a region established in accordance with Order No. 1000 of the Commission, entitled ‘Transmission Planning and Cost Allocation by Transmission Owning and Operating Public Utilities’ (76 Fed. Reg. 49842 (August 11, 2011)).

[p. 220, line 11](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=220) (indent level 2)

“(B) a description of the harm that those processes may be causing to wholesale or retail electric customers within the referring State; and

[p. 220, line 21](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=220) (indent level 3)
