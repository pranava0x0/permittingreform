## Summary

Bans any federal right of first refusal for transmission facilities selected in a regional or interregional plan for cost allocation, overriding any FERC rule or order to the contrary. ([p. 224, line 11 to p. 224, line 14](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=224))

## Key points

- Incumbent utilities get no federal first claim on regionally planned lines; competitive developers can bid ([p. 224, line 11 to p. 224, line 14](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=224))
