## Summary

Creates an expedited FERC license of 10 to 20 years for micro hydrokinetic projects of 5 megawatts or less (wave, tidal, current and free-flowing river turbines with no impoundment), with final action within 1 year of the application and categorical exclusions for low-disturbance activities. ([p. 358, line 21 to p. 358, line 23](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=358))

## Key points

- One-year licensing for hydrokinetic projects up to 5 MW ([p. 358, line 21 to p. 358, line 23](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=358))
