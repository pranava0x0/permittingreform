## Summary

Removes the federal drilling permit requirement for oil and gas wells on non-federal surface land where the federal government owns less than half the minerals in the drilling unit, or where the wellbore only enters or passes through federal minerals. Lessees notify Interior of state permits. Interior cannot require bonds, mitigation or reclamation approval on that non-federal land. Royalties are unchanged; Indian lands are excluded.

## Key points

- No federal permit to drill for wells on private or state surface with a minority federal mineral interest ([p. 287, line 1 to p. 287, line 4](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=287))
- State permits govern; royalties still owed ([p. 289, line 1 to p. 289, line 3](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=289))

### Current law

BLM requires an application for permit to drill whenever a well produces from a federal lease, wherever the surface is.

### EPRA 2024 (reported S. 4753)

Carried over from EPRA 2024 (sec. 203). EPRA's other onshore oil and gas changes, on lease nominations (sec. 201) and a 4-year drilling permit term (sec. 202), and its coal lease deadlines (sec. 204), are not in this bill.

### SPEED Act (House-passed H.R. 4776)

Not in the SPEED Act.

(a) IN GENERAL.—Notwithstanding the Mineral Leasing Act (30 U.S.C. 181 et seq.), the Federal Oil and Gas Royalty Management Act of 1982 (30 U.S.C. 1701 et seq.), or subpart 3162 of part 3160 of title 43, Code of Federal Regulations (or successor regulations), but subject to any applicable State or Tribal requirements and subsection (c), the Secretary of the Interior shall not require a permit to drill for an oil and gas lease under the Mineral Leasing Act (30 U.S.C. 181 et seq.) for an action occurring within an oil and gas drilling or spacing unit if—

[p. 287, line 1](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=287) (indent level 2)

(1) the well is located on non-Federal land and the Federal Government owns less than 50 percent of the oil and gas minerals within the oil and gas drilling or spacing unit;

[p. 287, line 5](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=287) (indent level 2)

(2) the well is located on non-Federal land overlying a non-Federal mineral estate, but some portion of the wellbore enters and produces from the Federal mineral estate subject to the lease; or

[p. 287, line 9](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=287) (indent level 2)

(3) the well is located on non-Federal land overlying a non-Federal mineral estate, but some portion of the wellbore traverses but does not produce from the Federal mineral estate subject to the lease.
