## Summary

Doubles the shelf life of programmatic environmental documents from 5 years to 10 years before an agency must reevaluate them. ([p. 62, line 1 to p. 62, line 2](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=62))

## Key points

- Programmatic documents can be relied on for 10 years, up from 5 ([p. 62, line 1 to p. 62, line 2](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=62))

### SPEED Act (House-passed H.R. 4776)

The SPEED Act (sec. 2(d)) made the same 5-to-10-year change and also let agencies rely on an earlier review for a substantially similar action; the Senate bill handles that through its exclusion for actions another agency already evaluated.
