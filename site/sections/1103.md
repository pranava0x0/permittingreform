## Summary

Requires every federal agency to update its NEPA regulations, procedures and policies within 180 days of enactment, directs the CEQ Chairman to standardize agency procedures, and lets the Chairman give technical assistance. ([p. 25, line 19 to p. 25, line 22](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=25))

## Key points

- 180-day deadline for agencies to conform their NEPA procedures ([p. 25, line 17 to p. 25, line 19](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=25))
- CEQ Chairman charged with standardizing procedures across agencies ([p. 26, line 2 to p. 26, line 3](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=26))

### Current law

NEPA section 103 currently requires agencies to review their authorities for conformity with NEPA; the 180-day implementation mandate and the standardization role are new.
