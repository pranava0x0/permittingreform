The Federal Energy Regulatory Commission may authorize employees of the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission to perform law enforcement duties as needed to ensure the safety of the Chairman and Commissioners of the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission in the performance of the official duties of the Chairman and Commissioners, respectively.
