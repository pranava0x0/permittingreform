## Summary

Shortens ESA section 7 formal consultation from 90 to 60 days, and the applicant-consent extension from 150 to 100 days. ([p. 134, line 16 to p. 134, line 17](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=134)) If consultation stretches past 145 days without the applicant's written consent, the applicant may sue under the APA for agency action unlawfully withheld. ([p. 135, line 23 to p. 135, line 24](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=135)) Consultation on any project with a NEPA document must finish by the NEPA deadline, unless the Secretary grants an extension for extraordinary circumstances. ([p. 136, line 10 to p. 136, line 13](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=136))

## Key points

- Formal consultation: 60 days, not 90 ([p. 134, line 16 to p. 134, line 17](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=134))
- Consultation must end when the NEPA document is due, absent an extension for extraordinary circumstances ([p. 136, line 10 to p. 136, line 13](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=136))
- APA remedy after 145 days ([p. 135, line 23 to p. 135, line 24](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=135))
