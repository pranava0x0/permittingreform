## Summary

Requires CEQ, with GSA, to run a public Environmental Review and Permitting Tracker listing every EIS project within 14 days of its notice of intent, with location, purpose and need, sponsor and agency contacts, quarterly schedule-compliance status explaining every slip and who caused it, and litigation status. ([p. 98, line 9 to p. 98, line 12](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=98))

## Key points

- Public tracker for every EIS project ([p. 98, line 2 to p. 98, line 3](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=98))
- Each schedule change must say whether the agency or the sponsor caused it ([p. 100, line 7 to p. 100, line 10](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=100))

[p. 97, line 20](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=97) (indent level 1)

(a) IN GENERAL.—The Chairman, in coordination with the General Services Administration, shall maintain an online database, to be known as the “Environmental Review and Permitting Tracker”, to track the status of Federal environmental reviews and authorizations for each transparency project.

[p. 98, line 1](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=98) (indent level 1)

(b) LIST OF TRANSPARENCY PROJECTS.—The Tracker shall include an up-to-date list of all transparency projects.

[p. 98, line 4](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=98) (indent level 1)

(c) CREATION OF TRACKER ENTRIES.—

[p. 98, line 5](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=98) (indent level 2)

(1) IN GENERAL.—The Chairman shall create a specific entry on the Tracker for a transparency project not later than—

[p. 98, line 8](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=98) (indent level 3)

(A) in the case of a transparency project described in section 1131(5)(A), the date that is 14 days after the date on which a notice of intent to prepare an environmental impact statement for that transparency project is published in the Federal Register in accordance with section 107(c) of the National Environmental Policy Act of 1969 (42 U.S.C. 4336a(c)); and

[p. 98, line 17](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=98) (indent level 3)

(B) in the case of a transparency project described in section 1131(5)(B), the date that is 13 months and 14 days after the deadline established for that transparency project under paragraph (1)(A) of section 107(g) of the National Environmental Policy Act of 1969 (42 U.S.C. 4336a(g)).

[p. 98, line 24](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=98) (indent level 2)

(2) SPECIFIC AND SEARCHABLE ENTRY.—Each entry of the Tracker shall include a specific and searchable entry for each transparency project listed on the Tracker in accordance with paragraph (1).

[p. 99, line 3](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=99) (indent level 2)

(3) EXCLUSIONS.—Any environmental review relating to an action taken by a Federal agency to develop a rule under subchapter II of chapter 5 and chapter 7 of title 5, United States Code (commonly known as “Administrative Procedure Act”), shall not be included on the Tracker.

[p. 99, line 9](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=99) (indent level 1)

(d) CONTENT OF TRACKER POSTINGS.—

[p. 99, line 10](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=99) (indent level 2)

(1) TRANSPARENCY PROJECTS.—For each transparency project added to the Tracker under subsection (c)(1), the Chairman shall post the most up-to-date information, including—

[p. 99, line 14](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=99) (indent level 3)

(A) a description of the location and major features of the transparency project;

[p. 99, line 16](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=99) (indent level 3)

(B) the statement of purpose and need for the transparency project, consistent with section 107(d) of the National Environmental Policy Act of 1969 (42 U.S.C. 4336a(d));

[p. 99, line 20](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=99) (indent level 3)

(C) contact information, including the email address, of—

[p. 99, line 22](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=99) (indent level 4)

(i) a representative of the project sponsor of the transparency project; and

[p. 99, line 24](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=99) (indent level 4)

(ii) a representative of the lead agency for the transparency project;

[p. 100, line 1](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=100) (indent level 3)

(D) the status of the compliance of each agency with respect to the applicable schedule and applicable deadlines for the transparency project, to be updated quarterly, including each modification of the applicable schedule, which shall describe—

[p. 100, line 7](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=100) (indent level 4)

(i) an explanation of each modification, including whether the modification is due to a delay on the part of an agency or the project sponsor;

[p. 100, line 11](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=100) (indent level 4)

(ii) the time period modified from the originally projected applicable schedule; and

[p. 100, line 14](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=100) (indent level 4)

(iii) any subsequent modifications from prior modifications, including whether such subsequent modification is due to a delay on the part of an agency or the project sponsor; and

[p. 100, line 19](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=100) (indent level 3)

(E) a description of the status of any litigation that is directly related to the transparency project, including, if practicable, any judicial document made available on an electronic docket maintained by a Federal, State, or local court.

[p. 101, line 1](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=101) (indent level 2)

(2) PUBLICATION.—All information described in this subsection shall be posted on the website of the Council not later than 7 days after the date on which the lead agency or the Chairman receives the information.
