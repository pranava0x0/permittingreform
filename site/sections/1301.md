## Summary

Defines 'best scientific and commercial data available' in the Endangered Species Act as all relevant, objective information at the time of the action, including models that account for error and are applied without policy presumptions.

## Key points

- First statutory definition of ESA's science standard ([p. 133, line 16 to p. 133, line 16](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=133))
