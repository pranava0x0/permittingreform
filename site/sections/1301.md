## Summary

Defines 'best scientific and commercial data available' in the Endangered Species Act as all relevant, objective information at the time of the action, including models that account for error and are applied without policy presumptions. ([p. 133, line 20 to p. 133, line 22](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=133))
