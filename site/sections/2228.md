## Summary

Directs Interior to identify standard geothermal procedures within 1 year and then publish, within 270 days, an updated and renamed 'Gold Book' of surface operating standards that covers geothermal, reviewed every 5 years. ([p. 347, line 6 to p. 347, line 9](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=347))

## Full statutory text

Extracted from the official PDF; line cites mark paragraph starts. Consult the PDF for exact layout.

[p. 346, line 19](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=346) (indent level 1)

(a) IDENTIFICATION.—Not later than 1 year after the date of enactment of this Act, the Secretary of the Interior, in consultation with other relevant Federal agencies, shall identify standard procedures and guidelines for efficient and environmentally responsible geothermal leasing and permitting to the extent such standard procedures and guidelines are not addressed in the fourth edition of the document of the Bureau of Land Management entitled “Surface Operating Standards and Guidelines for Oil and Gas Exploration and Development”, commonly known as the “Gold Book”, and most recently revised in 2007.

[p. 347, line 5](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=347) (indent level 1)

(b) PUBLICATION.—

[p. 347, line 6](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=347) (indent level 2)

(1) IN GENERAL.—Not later than 270 days after identifying standard procedures and guidelines under subsection (a), the Secretary of the Interior shall publish an updated version of the Gold Book, incorporating any changes necessary to support efficient and environmentally responsible geothermal leasing and permitting, for use by the field offices of the Bureau of Land Management and geothermal operators.

[p. 347, line 15](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=347) (indent level 2)

(2) RENAMING.—The Secretary of the Interior shall rename the Gold Book as the Secretary determines to be appropriate to reflect the incorporation of standard procedures and guidelines relating to geothermal development.

[p. 347, line 20](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=347) (indent level 1)

(c) CONSULTATION.—Before publishing an updated version of the Gold Book under subsection (b)(1), the Secretary of the Interior shall consult with—

[p. 347, line 23](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=347) (indent level 2)

(1) other relevant Federal agencies, including field offices of the Bureau of Land Management; and

[p. 348, line 1](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=348) (indent level 2)

(2) outside stakeholders, including developers and other experts.

[p. 348, line 3](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=348) (indent level 1)

(d) PERIODIC REVISION.—The Secretary of the Interior shall—

[p. 348, line 5](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=348) (indent level 2)

(1) not less frequently than once every 5 years, review the most recent version of the Gold Book (or a successor to that book); and

[p. 348, line 8](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=348) (indent level 2)

(2) as the Secretary of the Interior determines to be necessary to support efficient and environmentally responsible geothermal leasing and permitting, publish an updated version of the Gold Book (or a successor).

[p. 348, line 13](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=348) (indent level 1)

(e) INCLUSIONS.—Each updated version of the Gold Book (or a successor to that book) shall include standard procedures and guidelines for ensuring the efficient review and approval of environmentally responsible geothermal development, including—

[p. 348, line 18](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=348) (indent level 2)

(1) exploration and geophysical operations;

[p. 348, line 19](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=348) (indent level 2)

(2) permitting lease operations;

[p. 348, line 20](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=348) (indent level 2)

(3) compliance with all applicable laws (including regulations);

[p. 348, line 22](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=348) (indent level 2)

(4) construction and maintenance; and

[p. 348, line 23](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=348) (indent level 2)

(5) drilling, production, and utilization operations.
