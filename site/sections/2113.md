## Summary

Requires FERC to review every 5 years whether its fees and charges are enough to handle its workload promptly and report to Congress.

Part III of the Federal Power Act is amended by inserting after section 318 (as added by section 2106) the following:

[p. 278, line 7](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=278) (indent level 0)

“SEC. 318A. PERIODIC REVIEW OF FEES AND CHARGES.

[p. 278, line 8](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=278) (indent level 1)

“Not less frequently than once every 5 years, the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission shall—

[p. 278, line 10](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=278) (indent level 2)

“(1) undertake a review to determine whether the fees and charges the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission assesses under this Act and other laws, including section 3401 of the Omnibus Budget Reconciliation Act of 1986 (42 U.S.C. 7178), are sufficient to allow the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission to handle its workload in an expedient manner; and

[p. 278, line 18](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=278) (indent level 2)

“(2) submit to the Committee on Energy and Natural Resources of the Senate, the Committee on Energy and Commerce of the House of Representatives, the Committee on Appropriations of the Senate, and the Committee on Appropriations of the House of Representatives a report describing that review and the results of that review.”.
