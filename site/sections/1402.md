## Summary

Creates a cause of action for applicants in 46 named energy project types, plus any similar type a court certifies. The list runs from onshore and offshore wind, solar and storage to coal, gas, oil, nuclear, geothermal, transmission, pipelines, mining, hydrogen, hydropower, LNG and carbon storage.

The applicant must show a 'pattern of disparate treatment': an intentional, statistically demonstrated rise in denials or delays for that project type, compared with the prior 5 years. The pattern must last 180 days after enactment for permits without a NEPA document, or 280 days for those with one. Executive orders and official statements showing bias count as supporting evidence. The government must produce 5 years of raw statistics or face an adverse inference.

Permits for these projects that need no NEPA document must be decided within 1 year of a complete application.

Remedies are an injunction, fees, economic damages, and 50 to 100 percent of the expected construction cost, paid from the Judgment Fund. The applicant must finish the project or repay. Class actions are barred, and suits cannot be filed until 180 or 280 days after enactment.

- Executive orders and officials' statements admissible as evidence of bias ([p. 176, line 11 to p. 176, line 17](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=176))
