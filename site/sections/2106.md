## Summary

Requires FERC, within 18 months, to make each planning region file a consolidated interconnection and transmission planning process that looks 20 years ahead, publishes fuel-neutral planned interconnection locations with a fixed per-megawatt cost, and runs a cluster study with one decision point and nonrefundable security to deter speculative projects. Regions file within 2 years and update every 2 years.

## Key points

- Generation interconnection and transmission planned together ([p. 225, line 3 to p. 225, line 7](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=225))
- Published zonal per-megawatt interconnection prices ([p. 225, line 8 to p. 225, line 12](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=225))
- Nonrefundable deposits and withdrawal penalties for queue entrants ([p. 225, line 13 to p. 225, line 17](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=225))
