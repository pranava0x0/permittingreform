## Summary

No federal drilling permit for geothermal exploration and production on non-federal surface where the United States owns less than 50 percent of the subsurface geothermal estate, if the operator files its state permit with Interior. ([p. 338, line 20 to p. 338, line 23](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=338)) Indian lands are excluded; royalties are unchanged. ([p. 340, line 1 to p. 340, line 2](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=340))

## Key points

- State permits suffice for geothermal wells with a minority federal interest ([p. 338, line 20 to p. 338, line 23](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=338))
