## Summary

Creates a Geothermal Ombudsman and a Geothermal Permitting Task Force inside BLM within 60 days to resolve disputes between field offices and applicants, move staff to offices with backlogs, and pay retention allowances of up to 25 percent of basic pay.

## Key points

(a) DEFINITIONS.—In this section:

[p. 332, line 6](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=332) (indent level 2)

(2) GEOTHERMAL AUTHORIZATION.—The term “geothermal authorization” means any license, permit, approval, finding, determination, or other administrative decision issued by the Bureau (including any interagency consultation) that is required or authorized under Federal law in order to site, construct, reconstruct, or commence operation of a project that—

[p. 332, line 20](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=332) (indent level 3)

(B) uses geothermal energy to generate heat or electricity.

[p. 332, line 22](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=332) (indent level 2)

(3) GEOTHERMAL OMBUDSMAN.—The term “Geothermal Ombudsman” means the Geothermal Ombudsman appointed under subsection (b)(1).

[p. 333, line 1](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=333) (indent level 2)

(4) TASK FORCE.—The term “Task Force” means the Geothermal Permitting Task Force established under subsection (c)(1).

(b) GEOTHERMAL OMBUDSMAN.—
