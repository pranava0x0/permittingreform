## Summary

Requires EPA to use notice-and-comment rulemaking when it develops Clean Water Act water quality criteria, and routes challenges to those criteria and to any definition of 'waters of the United States' directly to the federal courts of appeals.

## Key points

- WOTUS definitions reviewed in courts of appeals, not district courts ([p. 103, line 7 to p. 103, line 9](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=103))
- Water quality criteria become rulemakings ([p. 102, line 23 to p. 102, line 25](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=102))
