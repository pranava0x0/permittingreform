## Summary

Amends the cap on Bonneville's funding of the Northwest Power and Conservation Council, section 4(c)(10)(B) of the Northwest Power Act, by adding the words 'since the date of enactment' of this bill. ### Current law

Bonneville may fund the Council up to 0.10 mill for each kilowatt-hour of firm power it forecasts selling that year.
