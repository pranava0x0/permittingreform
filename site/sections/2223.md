## Summary

Requires geothermal lease sales every year instead of every 2 years, with a replacement sale in the same year if one is cancelled, and sets 30-day completeness and 30-day decision deadlines for geothermal drilling permits. ([p. 329, line 18 to p. 329, line 21](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=329))

## Key points

- Annual geothermal lease sales ([p. 329, line 18 to p. 329, line 21](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=329))
- 30-day drilling permit clocks ([p. 330, line 3 to p. 330, line 5](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=330))

### Current law

Lease sales at least every 2 years; no permit deadlines.

### EPRA 2024 (reported S. 4753)

Carried over from EPRA 2024 (sec. 208(b) and (c)), with the completeness check lengthened from 10 to 30 days and a hard 10-day deadline added for deferred decisions.

### SPEED Act (House-passed H.R. 4776)

Not in the SPEED Act.
