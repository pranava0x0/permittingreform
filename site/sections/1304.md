## Summary

Creates ESA section 7A letting the Interior Secretary assign section 7 consultation duties to a state, by written agreement, for projects entirely inside the state. ([p. 138, line 7 to p. 138, line 10](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=138)) Interior must propose a rule within 270 days. ([p. 139, line 1 to p. 139, line 4](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=139)) States must show legal, financial and staffing capacity, waive sovereign immunity, accept federal court jurisdiction, keep public-records laws comparable to FOIA, and meet the same deadlines as the Secretary. ([p. 145, line 23 to p. 145, line 25](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=145)) Interior audits annually for 4 years, can terminate after a 120-day cure period, and applicants may still choose federal consultation. ([p. 149, line 1 to p. 149, line 2](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=149)) Interior's failure to act on an application within 180 days counts as a failure to act under the NEPA sanctions section. ([p. 144, line 20 to p. 144, line 23](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=144))

## Key points

- States can take over ESA consultation for projects within their boundaries ([p. 140, line 1 to p. 140, line 5](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=140))
- Applicants can opt out and consult with the Interior Department instead ([p. 148, line 7 to p. 148, line 10](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=148))
- Annual audits for 4 years; 10-year renewable agreements ([p. 146, line 22 to p. 146, line 23](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=146))
