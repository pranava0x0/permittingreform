## Summary

FERC, with the Energy Department, reports to Congress within 270 days on market barriers to hydropower and pumped storage and on how to pay hydropower for the grid services it provides, after a technical conference and public comment.

## Key points

- FERC report on hydropower market barriers ([p. 351, line 17 to p. 351, line 21](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=351))
