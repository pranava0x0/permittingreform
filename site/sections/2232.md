## Summary

Limits the mandatory conditions federal land agencies can attach to a hydropower license under Federal Power Act section 4(e) to those 'reasonably related to the effects of the project'. ([p. 354, line 8 to p. 354, line 10](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=354))
