## Summary

Directs the Energy Information Administration to revise Form 861 to collect electricity load from data center entities (AI, cloud, cryptocurrency) at least 90 days before the first April 1 after enactment.

## Key points

- First federal data series on data center electricity load ([p. 279, line 24 to p. 279, line 25](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=279))

[p. 279, line 3](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=279) (indent level 1)

(a) DEFINITION OF DATA CENTER ENTITY.—

[p. 279, line 4](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=279) (indent level 2)

(1) IN GENERAL.—In this section, the term “data center entity” means an end-user, or group of co-located end-users, the primary purpose of which is to house computing, networking, or data storage equipment used to process, store, or transmit digital data, including equipment used for—

[p. 279, line 10](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=279) (indent level 3)

(A) artificial intelligence training or inference;

[p. 279, line 12](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=279) (indent level 3)

(B) cloud computing or data hosting; or

[p. 279, line 13](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=279) (indent level 3)

(C) cryptocurrency mining or other distributed-ledger or digital-asset computation.

[p. 279, line 15](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=279) (indent level 2)

(2) EXCLUSION.—In this section, the term “data center entity” does not include computing or data storage equipment that is ancillary to a facility the primary purpose of which is commercial or industrial activity other than an activity described in paragraph (1).

[p. 279, line 21](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=279) (indent level 1)

(b) DATA COLLECTION.—Not later than 90 days before the first April 1 after the date of enactment of this Act, the Administrator of the Energy Information Administration shall revise Form 861 of the Energy Information Administration to include load from data center entities.
