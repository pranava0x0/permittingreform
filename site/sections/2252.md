## Summary

Directs Interior and Energy to identify preferred routes for offshore transmission cables within 2 years and review them every 3 years, makes Interior the lead agency for offshore transmission permitting, requires a final rule within 2 years, and lets Interior issue offshore transmission rights-of-way competitively or noncompetitively. ([p. 367, line 15 to p. 367, line 17](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=367))

## Key points

- Planned offshore transmission routes within 2 years ([p. 367, line 15 to p. 368, line 13](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=367))
- Interior is lead agency; rights-of-way can be noncompetitive ([p. 369, line 8 to p. 369, line 10](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=369))

### Current law

Each offshore transmission line is permitted on its own.

### EPRA 2024 (reported S. 4753)

Not in EPRA 2024, which made offshore lines eligible for FERC backstop siting and cost allocation (kept in section 2101).

### SPEED Act (House-passed H.R. 4776)

Not in the SPEED Act.
