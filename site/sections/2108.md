## Summary

Opens wholesale markets to aggregations of customer-sited batteries, fuel cells and generators that can respond within 15 minutes (5 kilowatts minimum per device, 100 kilowatts per aggregation). ([p. 240, line 23 to p. 240, line 24](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=240)) After 18 months, grid operator rules that block them are unjust and unreasonable. ([p. 243, line 13 to p. 243, line 15](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=243)) Grid operators file compliance tariffs within 180 days. ([p. 244, line 21 to p. 244, line 24](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=244)) Unless state law or the state regulator provides otherwise, distribution utilities in organized markets must interconnect these resources; for public power utilities and other section 201(f) entities the requirement is voluntary. ([p. 252, line 6 to p. 252, line 10](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=252)) They must also accept wholesale metering data. ([p. 253, line 7 to p. 253, line 10](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=253))

A device paid by a state retail program cannot be paid twice for the same service; ([p. 246, line 7 to p. 246, line 9](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=246)) FERC orders disgorgement and can impose penalties of up to 3 times the amount for each day it was knowingly kept. ([p. 250, line 16 to p. 250, line 20](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=250)) The Energy Department runs a voluntary streamlined local permitting program for rooftop solar, batteries and vehicle chargers, with $20 million a year authorized for fiscal years 2026 through 2031. ([p. 259, line 21 to p. 259, line 23](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=259))

## Key points

- Virtual power plants get statutory wholesale market access ([p. 243, line 20 to p. 243, line 22](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=243))
- Distribution utilities in organized markets must interconnect behind-the-meter resources ([p. 252, line 9 to p. 252, line 12](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=252))
- Device-level metering allowed; utilities cannot require their own equipment ([p. 241, line 16 to p. 241, line 19](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=241))
- Double-payment ban, with penalties of up to 3 times the amount per day ([p. 250, line 16 to p. 250, line 18](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=250))
- $20 million a year for streamlined local solar and storage permitting ([p. 259, line 22 to p. 259, line 23](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=259))

(e) ESTABLISHMENT OF PROGRAM TO FACILITATE VOLUNTARY STREAMLINED PROCESS FOR LOCAL PERMITTING OF QUALIFYING DISTRIBUTED ENERGY SYSTEMS.—

[p. 254, line 22](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=254) (indent level 2)

(1) DEFINITIONS.—In this subsection:

[p. 254, line 23](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=254) (indent level 3)

(A) AUTHORITY HAVING JURISDICTION.— The term “authority having jurisdiction” means any State, county, local, or Tribal office or official with jurisdiction—

[p. 255, line 3](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=255) (indent level 4)

(i) to issue permits relating to qualifying distributed energy systems;

[p. 255, line 5](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=255) (indent level 4)

(ii) to conduct inspections to enforce the requirements of a relevant code or standard relating to qualifying distributed energy systems; or

[p. 255, line 9](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=255) (indent level 4)

(iii) to approve the installation of, or the equipment and materials used in the installation of, qualifying distributed energy systems.

[p. 255, line 13](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=255) (indent level 3)

(B) QUALIFYING DISTRIBUTED ENERGY SYSTEM.—The term “qualifying distributed energy system” means any equipment or materials installed in, on, or near a building or premises to support onsite or local energy use, including equipment or materials, the primary function of which is—

[p. 255, line 20](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=255) (indent level 4)

(i) to generate electricity from distributed renewable energy sources, including solar photovoltaic systems or similar solar energy technologies;

[p. 256, line 1](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=256) (indent level 4)

(ii) to store and discharge electricity from batteries with a capacity of at least 2 kilowatt hours;

[p. 256, line 4](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=256) (indent level 4)

(iii) to charge a plug-in electric drive vehicle at a power rate of at least 2 kilowatts; or

[p. 256, line 7](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=256) (indent level 4)

(iv) to refuel a hydrogen fuel cell electric vehicle.

[p. 256, line 9](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=256) (indent level 3)

(C) SECRETARY.—The term “Secretary” means the Secretary of Energy.

[p. 256, line 11](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=256) (indent level 2)

(2) PROGRAM.—Not later than 180 days after the date of enactment of this Act, the Secretary, in consultation with trade associations and other entities representing distributed energy system installers, organizations representing State, local, and Tribal governments engaged in permitting, and other interested stakeholders, shall carry out a program to further develop, expand, and support the adoption of a voluntary streamlined permitting and inspection process for authorities having jurisdiction to use for the permitting of qualifying distributed energy systems.

[p. 256, line 23](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=256) (indent level 2)

(3) ACTIVITIES OF THE PROGRAM.—In carrying out the program established under paragraph (2), the Secretary shall—

[p. 257, line 1](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=257) (indent level 3)

(A) further develop and expand an exemplary streamlined permitting process that includes an online permitting platform—

[p. 257, line 4](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=257) (indent level 4)

(i) for expediting, standardizing, and streamlining permitting; and

[p. 257, line 6](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=257) (indent level 4)

(ii) that authorities having jurisdiction may voluntarily use to receive, review, and approve permit applications relating to qualifying distributed energy systems;

[p. 257, line 10](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=257) (indent level 3)

(B) establish targets for the adoption of a streamlined, expedited permitting process by authorities having jurisdiction;

[p. 257, line 13](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=257) (indent level 3)

(C) provide technical assistance and training directly or indirectly to authorities having jurisdiction on using and adopting the exemplary streamlined permitting process described in subparagraph (A), including the adoption of any necessary building codes;

[p. 257, line 19](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=257) (indent level 3)

(D) develop a voluntary inspection protocol and related tools to expedite, standardize, and streamline the inspection of qualifying distributed energy systems, including—

[p. 257, line 23](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=257) (indent level 4)

(i) by investigating the potential for using remote inspections;

[p. 258, line 1](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=258) (indent level 4)

(ii) by investigating the potential for sample-based inspection for distributed energy system installers with a demonstrated track record of high-quality work; and

[p. 258, line 5](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=258) (indent level 4)

(iii) by investigating opportunities to integrate the voluntary inspection protocol into the online permitting platform described in subparagraph (A) and the platforms of government software providers; and

[p. 258, line 11](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=258) (indent level 3)

(E) take any other action to expedite, standardize, streamline, or improve the process for permitting, inspecting, or interconnecting qualifying distributed energy systems.

[p. 258, line 15](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=258) (indent level 2)
