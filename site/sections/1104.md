## Summary

Conforming edits to NEPA section 102(2): agencies must develop methods consistent with data and technology standards for eNEPA technologies, and 'responsible official' becomes 'lead agency'. ([p. 27, line 1 to p. 27, line 4](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=27))

## Key points

- Ties agency NEPA methods to eNEPA technology standards ([p. 27, line 1 to p. 27, line 4](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=27))
