## Summary

Conforming cross-references in the Federal Power Act and a savings clause: nothing in the transmission title gives FERC authority over retail electricity sales or local distribution. ([p. 280, line 18 to p. 280, line 21](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=280))

## Key points

- No new FERC authority over retail sales or local distribution ([p. 280, line 18 to p. 280, line 21](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=280))
