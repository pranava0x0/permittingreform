## Summary

Adds a 150-day statute of limitations for challenges to section 404 permits, general permits, verifications and state program approvals. ([p. 129, line 10 to p. 129, line 12](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=129)) Where a draft was published with at least 30 days for comment, a plaintiff must have commented on the issue. ([p. 131, line 12 to p. 131, line 14](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=131)) Remedies are limited to remand, with vacatur or injunction available only on a finding of imminent and substantial danger to health or the environment. ([p. 132, line 9 to p. 132, line 14](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=132)) Remand deadlines are capped at 180 days. ([p. 133, line 4 to p. 133, line 5](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=133))

## Key points

- 150-day limit on 404 permit suits ([p. 129, line 21 to p. 129, line 23](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=129))
- Permits stay in force on remand absent imminent and substantial danger ([p. 132, line 9 to p. 132, line 13](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=132))
