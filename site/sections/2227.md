## Summary

Lets Interior charge geothermal applicants and lessees for the cost of processing and inspections through September 30, 2033, with hardship reductions, and requires a report within 5 years.

## Key points

- Geothermal cost recovery through fiscal year 2033 ([p. 343, line 1 to p. 343, line 5](https://pranava0x0.github.io/permittingreform/bill.pdf#page=343))

### Current law

New authority.

### EPRA 2024 (reported S. 4753)

EPRA 2024 (sec. 208(d)) told Interior to write cost recovery rules within 180 days. This bill grants the authority directly, with a 2033 sunset.

### SPEED Act (House-passed H.R. 4776)

Not in the SPEED Act.
