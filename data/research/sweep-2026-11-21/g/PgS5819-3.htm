                    AMENDMENTS SUBMITTED AND PROPOSED

       SA 7028. Mrs. MOODY (for herself and Ms. Hassan) proposed 
     an amendment to the bill H.R. 1520, to prohibit 
     discrimination on the basis of mental or physical disability 
     in cases of organ transplants.
       SA 7029. Mrs. GILLIBRAND (for Mr. Cruz (for himself and Ms. 
     Rosen)) proposed an amendment to the bill H.R. 4624, to amend 
     the Professional Boxing Safety Act of 1996 to establish 
     requirements for unified boxing organizations, to further 
     enhance the well-being of professional boxers, and for other 
     purposes.
       SA 7030. Mr. LEE (for himself, Mr. Heinrich, Mrs. Capito, 
     and Mr. Whitehouse) submitted an amendment intended to be 
     proposed to amendment SA 6883 proposed by Mr.

[[Page S5820]]

     Thune (for Mrs. Capito (for herself, Mr. Lee, Mr. Whitehouse, 
     and Mr. Heinrich)) to the bill H.R. 2347, to amend the 
     Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to exclude from gross income 
     any damages, other than punitive damages, received on account 
     of any sexual acts or sexual contact; which was ordered to 
     lie on the table.
       SA 7031. Mr. SCHIFF (for himself and Mr. Curtis) proposed 
     an amendment to the bill S. 4035, to amend the Commodity 
     Exchange Act to prohibit the listing of contracts relating to 
     war, death, and similar activities.
       SA 7032. Mr. MORENO (for Mrs. Blackburn) proposed an 
     amendment to the bill S. 2479, to amend the charter of the 
     Reserve Organization of the United States, and for other 
     purposes.
       SA 7033. Mr. MORENO (for Mr. Peters) proposed an amendment 
     to the bill S. 580, to require the Secretary of Commerce to 
     provide training and guidance relating to violations of human 
     rights, including such abuses perpetrated against Uyghur and 
     other religious and ethnic minority groups by the Government 
     of the People's Republic of China, and for other purposes.
       SA 7034. Mr. MORENO (for Mr. Peters) proposed an amendment 
     to the bill S. 580, supra.
