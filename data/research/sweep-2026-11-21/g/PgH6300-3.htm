                     OPPOSITION TO PERMITTING DEAL

  (Ms. STANSBURY asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. STANSBURY. Mr. Speaker, I rise once again to oppose the Senate 
permitting deal--a deal cut behind closed doors without public 
hearings, without Tribal consultation, and without public input. It is 
opposed by all major environmental organizations, civil rights and good 
government organizations, Tribal nations across New Mexico and the 
country, State Governors, legislators, and attorneys general.
  No matter how they try to spin it, this bill, even as amended, 
undermines Tribal sovereignty; undercuts the Clean Water Act, NEPA, and 
the Endangered Species Act; locks in dangerous oil and gas and mining 
projects that could threaten sacred sites; and that could, yes, indeed 
be used to support Trump in building his arch and vanity projects.
  Not only does it threaten our public lands and waters, we know that 
Donald Trump plans to use it to build data centers because he said so 
himself.
  We appreciate the work that has gone into making this bill better, 
but this is not the bill. This is not the time, and this is not what 
the American people asked for. Vote ``no.''
